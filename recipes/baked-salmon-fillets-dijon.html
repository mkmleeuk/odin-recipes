Baked Salmon Fillets

Ingredients

  • 4oz fillets salmon
  • 3 tablespoons prepared dijon mustard
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup italian-style dry bread crumbs
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees. Line a shallow baking pan with aluminium foil.
  2. Place salmon fillets skin-side down to prepared baking pan. Spread a thin layer of msutard on top of each fillet: season with salt and pepper
  3. Top with bread crumbs, then drizzle with melted butter.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until salmon flakes easily with a fork, about 15 minutes.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
